Rain Check

Wind or swell wiped out tomorrow? Rebook the whole day from your phone in minutes, not a whole evening.

Every group that never rebooks is a lesson you don't get paid for. Rain Check keeps them in one list until they're sorted.

How it works

  1. Cancel a session with one tap

    Tap Cancel, confirm with a big button, and every group in that session goes onto your Rebook list.

  2. Move each group with one tap

    Each group shows the next three sessions with room for everyone. Tap one to move them. Tapped the wrong one? Hit Undo.

  3. See the money still waiting

    One screen shows how much lesson money is still waiting to be rebooked, so you know when the job is done.
